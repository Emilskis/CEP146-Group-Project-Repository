Introduction

Cybersecurity is the practice of defending computers, network, and digital data from attacks or unauthorized access. In today's connected world, people depend heavily on technology for communication, business, and daily life. This reliance
also incraeses the risk of cyber threats such as hacking, phishing, and malware. The main goal of cybersecurity is to protect information by maintaining its confidentiality, integrity, and availablity. Effectively protection involves not
technology but also user awareness and strong security policies. As digital systems evolve, so do the challenges, making cybersecurity an essential field for ensuring privacy, trust, and safety in our modern society.
